Averaged answer

calibrated confidence; low confidence goes to an inspector

Size, colour, defects, skin and weight are measured directly from the fruit outline and pixels. A printed 30 mm marker in the photo gives sizes in millimetres.

Grade uses an ensemble of two CNNs (MobileNetV3 + EfficientNetV2-B0) · 6

Two different kinds of model, a transformer pretrained on a huge image collection and a convolutional network trained on our date photos, make different mistakes; averaging them is more reliable than either. One DINOv3 pass serves both variety and ripeness, so the extra cost is small. The confidence is calibrated (temperature scaling) so a low score really means "check this one".
