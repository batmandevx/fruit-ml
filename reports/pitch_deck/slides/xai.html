Grad-CAM colours the parts of the photo that drove the decision. We don't just show pretty heatmaps; we test them. The model-randomisation test (Adebayo et al., NeurIPS 2018) checks the map comes from what the model learned. The deletion test checks that hiding the hot regions really changes the prediction. Fruit focus and the background-removal test check the model looks at the fruit. Every production model passes; the full galleries are in the evaluation report.
