Trust · a problem we caught

Explainability exposed a hidden shortcut before it reached production

first DINOv3 model, normal photos

81%

98.5%

fixed model, background removed

The first model partly recognised each variety's photo session, not the fruit. We retrained it on every photo twice, with and without background. Accuracy on normal photos stayed at 99.5%.

"Shortcut learning", Geirhos et al., Nature Machine Intelligence 2020 · 13

This is the strongest trust story in the deck. A model that scores 99% by recognising the photo studio would fail on the company's photos. The Grad-CAM heatmaps showed heat on the background; we confirmed it by removing the background (accuracy fell to 81%, while the CNN only fell from 96.8% to 95.6%). The fix: train on background-removed copies too. The fixed model holds 98.5% without background, and the final test accuracy is unchanged at 99.5%. The choice was made on validation before the test set was scored.
