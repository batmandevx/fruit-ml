Licensing · open items

Two items to settle before launch, each with a ready fix

1 · Ripeness training data

The Moroccan orchard dataset has no licence, so commercial rights stay with its authors (Zarouit, Zekkouri, Ouhda, Aksasse, 2023).

Fix: ask the authors for written permission, or retrain ripeness on the company's own photos, which the pilot collects anyway.

2 · Legal sign-off on DINOv3

The DINOv3 License is Meta's own, not a standard open-source licence, so counsel should review it once.

Fallback: DINOv2 is Apache 2.0. Its probe matched DINOv3's 99.0% variety accuracy on validation.

Be upfront about these; it builds trust. Ripeness: the model is a prototype until the data question is settled. Retraining on the dealer's own fruit is also the main accuracy fix for ripeness, so the pilot solves both. DINOv3: the licence clearly allows commercial use, but company policy may require legal review of non-standard licences. If legal prefers a standard licence, switching to DINOv2 (Apache 2.0) costs no measurable variety accuracy on validation (both 99.0%), but uses a larger model (ViT-L, 300M parameters).
