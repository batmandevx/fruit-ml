Dataset licences read from the Zenodo, Kaggle and Mendeley records on 1 Oct 2026 · 10

We checked every piece, not just DINOv3. All software is under permissive open-source licences (Apache 2.0, BSD). Three of the four datasets are CC BY 4.0: commercial use allowed with credit to the authors (credits are on the references slide). The fourth, the Moroccan ripeness dataset, publishes no licence at all. Without a licence the authors keep all rights by default, so the ripeness model needs their permission or retraining before commercial use.
