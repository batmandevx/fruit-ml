Why DINOv3

A state-of-the-art vision model, used as a ready-made eye

Released by Meta AI, August 2025

Siméoni et al., "DINOv3", arXiv:2508.10104. Code and weights public on GitHub and Hugging Face.

Self-supervised on the LVD-1689M web image set

Learned from images alone, no labels: general visual features that transfer to new domains.

We use the ViT-B/16 version, 86M parameters

Frozen, with a small classifier on top: trains in minutes on a laptop GPU from a few thousand photos.

"…a versatile vision foundation model that outperforms the specialized state of the art across a broad range of settings, without fine-tuning."

DINOv3 paper abstract, 2025

On our validation data: DINOv3 probe alone 99.0% vs best fine-tuned CNN 97.6% (variety) · 7

Plain-language version: instead of teaching a network to see from scratch with our few thousand date photos, we reuse a model that already learned general vision from a very large image collection, and only train a small final layer for dates. That is why accuracy jumped while training took minutes. We also tested DINOv2 (Large) and DINOv3 (Large); the Base model was as accurate and far smaller, so we use it.
